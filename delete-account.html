Account & Data Deletion

Delete your MechaniCar account.

Last updated: May 31, 2026

1. What gets deleted

When you delete your MechaniCar account, we permanently remove the following within 24 hours:

  • Your name, email, phone number, and password hash
  • Your business profile (if you're a repair shop)
  • Your saved location and search radius
  • Photos, videos, and voice notes you uploaded
  • Private chat messages you sent
  • Push notification tokens and notification preferences
  • Stored credit card / billing customer association (Stripe customer record is also closed)

2. What we keep (anonymized)

For fraud prevention, dispute resolution, and tax/legal compliance, we keep anonymized versions of:

  • Job posts and quotes you participated in (with your name and identifying info removed)
  • Star ratings and reviews you left for shops (shown under a deleted-user handle like "Owner-XXXX")
  • Billing transaction records required by tax law (Stripe and Google Play also retain billing history per their own retention policies)

None of this retained data can be used to identify you.

3. How to delete your account — in-app

  1. Open the MechaniCar app on your phone, or visit mechanicar.app in a browser
  2. Sign in to your account
  3. Tap Settings (gear icon in the top-right of the app)
  4. Tap the Privacy tab
  5. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account
  6. Confirm by typing your email address in the prompt
  7. Tap Permanently delete

You'll be signed out immediately and your data will be erased from our systems within 24 hours. You'll receive a confirmation email when the deletion is complete.

This action is irreversible. Once your account is deleted, your job posts disappear from active shop browsing, any pending quotes are voided, and you can't recover your data. If you only want to cancel a subscription, do that first via Settings → Subscription and keep your account active.

4. How to delete your account — email request

If you can no longer sign in (lost password, lost device, etc.), email us and we'll handle deletion manually:

  • To: privacy@mechanicar.app
  • Subject: "Account deletion request"
  • Body: Include the email address you registered with so we can verify ownership.

We respond within 1 business day and complete the deletion within 7 days of verification.

5. Cancel your subscription before deleting

If you have an active paid subscription (Pro $59.99/mo or Premium $79.99/mo), deleting your account will not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription. Always cancel first:

  • Android (Google Play): Open the Google Play app → tap your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → MechaniCar → Cancel subscription.
  • iOS (Apple): Open the Settings app → tap your Apple ID at the top → Subscriptions → MechaniCar → Cancel subscription.
  • Web (Stripe): In the MechaniCar web app, go to Settings → Subscription → Manage subscription.
