Privacy Policy

Your data, your call.

Last updated: May 30, 2026

1. Who we are

MechaniCar ("we", "us", "our") operates the MechaniCar marketplace at mechanicar.app and the corresponding iOS and Android apps. We connect car owners with local repair shops via anonymous job posts and private quotes.

2. What we collect

  • Account info: name, email, password hash, role (owner / shop / admin), and shop name + business application data if applicable.
  • Location: approximate coordinates and the search radius you set, used for matching. You may use Google sign-in (when offered) or set your location manually.
  • Job posts & quotes: photos, videos, vehicle details (make / model / year), descriptions, prices, and time estimates.
  • Messages: private 1-on-1 threads between owners and shops.
  • Billing: Stripe handles all web payment information; Apple App Store / Google Play handle all native-app payment information. We store only the customer / subscription / receipt IDs and the subscription status.
  • Device data: IP address, user agent, push notification token (only if you opt in), and notification permission state.

3. How we use it

  • Match nearby repair shops to owner job posts (using your radius and service categories).
  • Deliver private quotes, bookings, and messages between you and your counterparty.
  • Authenticate you and prevent abuse.
  • Send transactional emails (job updates, quote notifications, trial expiry reminders, password resets).
  • Send web and mobile push notifications that you explicitly opt into.
  • Process subscription payments via Stripe (web) and Google Play / Apple (native apps).

4. What we DON'T do

  • We don't sell your data. Ever.
  • We don't share owner identity with shops until you explicitly accept a quote.
  • We don't run third-party advertising trackers.
  • We don't train AI models on your data.

5. Third parties we use

  • Stripe — subscription billing on the web (PCI-compliant payment processor).
  • Apple App Store / Google Play Billing — subscription billing inside the native iOS & Android apps. When you subscribe through the native apps, Apple or Google processes your payment and shares only receipt validation data with us (never your card details).
  • RevenueCat — handles native subscription receipt validation and entitlement state on our behalf.
  • Resend — transactional email delivery.
  • OpenStreetMap / Leaflet — location picker map tiles (no personal data is shared with the tile provider).
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) / Apple Push Notification service (APNs) — push notification delivery. Only the device endpoint identifier (not the notification body) is shared with these services.

6. Your rights

You can at any time:

  • Export your data — email privacy@mechanicar.app with the subject "Data export request".
  • Delete your account — open Settings → Privacy → "Delete account". We remove your personal data within 24 hours and anonymize historical records so existing audit trails remain valid.
  • Revoke push permission — Settings → Notifications, or revoke it directly in your browser / device settings.
  • Cancel your subscription — "Manage subscription" in Settings opens the Stripe Customer Portal (web) or your Google Play / Apple subscription management page (native).

7. Data retention

Active account data is retained while your account is alive. After deletion, personal identifiers are removed within 24 hours and only anonymized historical records (used for fraud prevention and audit purposes) remain. Stripe and Google Play retain billing history per their own retention policies.

8. Children

MechaniCar is intended for users 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a child has provided data to MechaniCar, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. International data transfers

MechaniCar is operated from the United States. By using the service, you consent to the transfer and processing of your data in the United States. We rely on the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission for any EU-to-US data transfers.
